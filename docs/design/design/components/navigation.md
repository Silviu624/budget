# Navigation

Four destinations — Sumar, Fonduri, Istoric, Setări — as a bottom tab bar on mobile and a left sidebar on desktop (from `breakpoint-desktop`, 960px).

- Tab bar: `size-tabbar` (64px) on `surface` with a `line` top border; active tab in `accent` with an `accent-soft` pill behind the icon; labels in `micro`.
- Sidebar: `size-sidebar` (240px), wordmark „Buget” on top, items 40px tall with `radius-sm`; active item `accent-soft` / `accent`. The couple's account sits at the bottom.
- The app header always carries „Buget” and „Deconectare” (text button with logout icon); on desktop „Deconectare” sits at the right of the top bar.
