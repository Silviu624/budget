# Icons

One small set of 24px line icons drawn for Buget — 1.75 stroke, round caps and joins, `currentColor`.

- Use `Buget.icon(name, size)`; names are shown on each tile. Sizes: 22 in the tab bar, 20 in navigation and headers, 16–18 in buttons and rows, 14 in notes and chips.
- Kind icons: `vault` = Fond de economii (on `accent-soft`), `wallet` = Buget de cheltuieli (on `surface-sunken`).
- Never fill icons with green or red; they take the colour of their text.
