# Movement row

A line of „Istoric mișcări” on the bucket detail: date, type, amount, note.

- Dates are `dd.mm.yyyy` (`02.09.2026`).
- Contribution: `+1.400,00 €` in `positive`; Retragere: `−600,00 €` in `negative`; Sold inițial: neutral.
- The month's planned contribution sits on top on `surface-hover`, amount in `ink-muted`, until „Aplică luna”.
