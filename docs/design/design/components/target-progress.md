# Target progress

The progress bar of a saving bucket towards its „Țintă”.

- **Provide**: balance and target. Shows `pct din țintă` and „mai sunt X €” beneath.
- Fill is always `accent`, never green — green is reserved for money amounts. At 100% the caption becomes „Țintă atinsă” with a check.
- 8px tall (10px on the bucket detail hero), `radius-full`, track `surface-sunken`.
- A bucket without a target shows „Fără țintă” instead of a bar.
